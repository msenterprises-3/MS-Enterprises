Quick Admin Instructions

Welcome to the MS Furniture Gallery Catalog Dashboard! Here you can control all details visible on the frontend catalogue website:

  • Manage Products: Add new furniture items, set retail vs offer price badges, configure specifications, and upload multiple catalog images.
  • Category Organization: Map products to specific categories and subcategories to streamline customer browsing.
  • Sliders & Promotion: Change banners, promo offer countdown timers, and manage custom badges.
  • Customer Proofs: Showcase testimonials and review videos directly on the homepage.
  • System Settings: Update your WhatsApp number, contact details, coordinates, and default site SEO description.
